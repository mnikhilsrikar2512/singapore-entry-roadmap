Most common mistake: remitting share capital to Singapore before the AD bank has accepted Form ODI Part I and issued a UIN. That is a FEMA contravention that needs compounding. Do Phase 1 before any money moves.
Corporate shareholder note: the Start-Up Tax Exemption needs at least one individual shareholder with 10% or more. A Singapore company 100% owned by your Indian company does not qualify; it gets the Partial Tax Exemption instead (75% of the first S$10k, 50% of the next S$190k).

    Tax & transfer pricing essentials

    How money moves between the two companies, and the rules that apply to each flow.

    Share capital India → SGNo tax. Issue shares and update register of membersODI under FEMA. Form ODI Part I before remitting; evidence of investment to AD bank within 6 monthsKeep the UIN, SWIFT copy and share certificate together
    Services fee SG pays India for delivery workDeductible expense. No Singapore withholding when services are performed wholly outside SingaporeExport of services: GST zero-rated under LUT.     Dividend SG → IndiaOne-tier system; no withholding tax on dividendsTaxable in India at normal rates; repatriate within 90 days of receipt under ODI rulesReport in APR; check foreign tax credit position with your CA
    Royalty / licence SG pays India for IPWithholding tax; India–Singapore DTAA caps it at 10%Taxable; credit for Singapore WHTOnly if IP stays in India. Do not move IP to Singapore without a valuation and tax advice
    Staff secondmentEP needed if working in Singapore; Singapore payroll tax rules applyPermanent establishment risk if Indian staff conclude contracts for SG, or vice versaUse a secondment agreement; keep decision-making for each company in its own country
    Keep management in Singapore. Hold board meetings in Singapore (or with the chair in Singapore), keep minutes there and let the resident director take real decisions. This supports Singapore tax residency (needed for a Certificate of Residence and DTAA benefits) and avoids the company being treated as Indian-resident under India's Place of Effective Management test.
