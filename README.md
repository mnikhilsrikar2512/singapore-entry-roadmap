# Singapore Entry Roadmap

An interactive, single-page playbook for an Indian IT company setting up a Singapore private limited subsidiary.

**Live page:** https://mnikhilsrikar2512.github.io/singapore-entry-roadmap/

## What's inside

- At-a-glance facts and structure comparison (subsidiary vs branch vs representative office)
- Set-up partner comparison (ScaleX Business, CorporateServices.com and alternatives)
- 7-phase roadmap (~16 weeks) with 54 tick-off checklist items (progress saved in your browser)
- 9 sample formats: ODI board resolution, ACRA data sheet, intercompany agreement term sheet, EP data sheet, year-1 budget, bank account pack, TP invoice working, advisor RFP, RACI
- Year-1 cost estimator (SGD and INR)
- Annual compliance calendar for Singapore and India
- Tax and transfer pricing essentials, government portal links

Rules checked as of 5 October 2026. This is a planning aid, not legal or tax advice.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step.
